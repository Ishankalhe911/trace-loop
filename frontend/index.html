How the chain works

Every device moves through a strict state machine enforced by a smart contract. No step can be skipped.

1

Register with proof

The first buyer registers the device with a GST invoice or warranty receipt. KYC-verified identity anchors the chain.

2

Brand-authorized stamp

A Dell, HP, or Lenovo service centre physically inspects the device and issues a tamper-evident on-chain stamp.

3

Verified transfer

Every resale passes through the same verifiable node. Buyer-to-buyer transfers are blocked at the smart contract level.

4

Declared hardware changes

RAM swapped? SSD upgraded? Declare it. Any change invalidates the prior stamp — re-verification required before sale.

5

Dispute resolution

Any party can raise a dispute. Disputes permanently attach to the device's history — they can't be removed or overwritten.

6

CPCB-registered recycling

The lifecycle ends at a CPCB-registered facility. The terminal state is written to the Algorand ledger. Permanent. Irreversible.
